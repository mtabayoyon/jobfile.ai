The problem

The job happens in the field. The record happens later, if at all.

Restoration, plumbing, HVAC, electrical and roofing companies run on what their techs see on site. Most of it lives in phones, truck cabs and memory, and the office retypes what does get written down into job, estimating, accounting and CRM systems.

Our approach

Capture once, hands-free. Let AI do the paperwork.

Techs wear a camera and talk through the job. Jobfile.ai turns the footage and voice into structured job records, equipment logs and daily digests, flags anything uncertain for a person to confirm, and sends clean data to the systems the company already runs.

Where we are

Built inside a working service company.

Jobfile.ai started as the system behind Allied Restoration Company, a water, fire and mold restoration and equipment rental operator in the San Francisco Bay Area. Allied is the first operator running on it, and early pilots with other service companies are next.
