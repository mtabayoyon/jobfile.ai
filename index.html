The problem

The job happens in the field. The record happens later, if at all.

  • Field knowledge lives in phones, truck cabs and memory.When a tech is off or moves on, what they saw goes with them.
  • The office retypes the same job again and again.Job file, estimate, invoice and CRM each get their own copy, entered by hand.
  • Photos and readings lose their context.Which room, which day, which wall, which meter. Without it, they don't hold up.
  • Owners run the business on what got entered.Not on what actually happened on site.

How it works

Capture once. Structure it. Put it where the work gets run.

  1. Capture

    Techs work the way they already do. A walk-through video, a few photos, a quick voice note, a meter reading, a customer call. No long forms on a phone.

    videophotosvoice notesreadingscalls

  2. Structure

    AI pulls out the facts: rooms, materials, readings, equipment, scope and follow-ups. Anything it isn't sure of is flagged for a person to confirm before it goes anywhere.

    scopematerialsequipmentfollow-ups

  3. Push

    Clean records go into the systems your office already uses, and the people who run the business get a daily digest of every open job.

Other field service

What changes

Less chasing, less retyping, better decisions.

  • Estimates start from what was found. Scope comes from the walk-through, not from memory a day later.
  • The office stops retyping. One capture feeds the job file, the estimate and the invoice.
  • Equipment is tracked where it was left. Every placement and pickup is logged against the job.
  • Job files that hold up. Dated photos, readings and notes, organized for customers and carriers.
  • A daily picture of every job. Owners and managers see what happened without calling the crew.
  • Knowledge stays with the company. What your best techs know becomes part of your records.
